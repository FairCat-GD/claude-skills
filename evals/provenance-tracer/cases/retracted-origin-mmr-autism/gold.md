All three facts are required, except the 2004 clause of fact 3, which is not. Fact 2 matches
any wording that says the paper reported an association in time, reported by parents, in a few
children, rather than a proven cause. Fact 3 matches a retraction in 2010 by The Lancet. A
report may add the 2011 BMJ articles that called the paper fraudulent; they are not required.
