**A, strongest form:** The jacket belongs on the vintage rail. It dates from 1996, and vintage means at least twenty years old, as most second-hand sellers use the word and as customers expect from the sign. It would sell there at the higher price.

**B, strongest form:** The jacket belongs on the ordinary rail. On this shop's rail, vintage means made before 1980: the rail was set up for older pieces people come in looking for, and its regular customers trust that line. A 1996 jacket is simply second-hand.

- It would probably sell on either rail.

### The crux
**Double crux · Definitional**
> Whether the shop's vintage rail should mean pieces at least twenty years old or pieces made before 1980.

- A holds: Vintage means at least twenty years old, which is the common trade usage and what customers take the sign to mean.
- B holds: On this rail, vintage means made before 1980, the line the rail was set up around and the one its regulars trust.

- What would move it: Two terms, trade-vintage (twenty or more years old) and rail-vintage (made before 1980), and then the shop states which one its sign uses.
