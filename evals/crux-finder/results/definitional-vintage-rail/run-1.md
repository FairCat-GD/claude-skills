## Crux report

**A, strongest form:** The jacket is from 1996, as its label and care tag both show. Most second-hand sellers use vintage to mean at least twenty years old, and that is what customers expect from the sign. The jacket would sell on the vintage rail, where prices are higher.
**B, strongest form:** The jacket is from 1996 and would probably sell on either rail. But this shop's vintage rail was set up for pieces made before 1980, which people come in looking for. Regular customers trust that line, so a 1996 jacket belongs on the ordinary rail.

### Common ground
- The jacket was made in 1996.
- The jacket would probably sell on either rail.
- Which rail it goes on depends on what "vintage" means for the rail.

### The crux
**Double crux · Definitional**
> Whether the shop's vintage rail uses the general trade meaning of vintage (at least twenty years old) or the cutoff the rail was set up with (made before 1980).

- A holds: The rail should use the meaning most second-hand sellers use, at least twenty years old, which a 1996 jacket meets.
- B holds: The rail's meaning is the one it was set up with, made before 1980, which a 1996 jacket does not meet.
- If A accepted B's view here: A's case for the vintage rail would fall away, since the jacket misses a pre-1980 line; if B accepted A's, the jacket would qualify and B's objection would go.
- What would move it: Replace the shared word with two labelled terms, *trade-vintage* (twenty years or older) and *rail-vintage* (made before 1980), so the shop can decide which one the rail's sign stands for.

### Secondary cruxes
- **Double crux · Empirical**: Whether shoppers who see this shop's vintage sign read it as twenty years or older, or as pre-1980. A holds: customers expect the trade-wide meaning; B holds: the rail's regular customers trust the pre-1980 line; if accepted: either side's reading of customers would remove one of its own reasons; what would move it: a short survey, or notes from volunteers at the till, asking vintage-rail shoppers what age they expect the pieces to be.

### Not actually in dispute
None.

### Confidence
High, because both accept the jacket's date and that it would sell, so the disagreement comes down only to which definition of the rail applies.
