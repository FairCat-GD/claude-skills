## Crux report

**A, strongest form:** The jacket should go on the vintage rail, because it was made in 1996, as its label and care tag both show. Vintage means at least twenty years old, which is how most second-hand sellers use the word and what customers expect when they see the sign. The jacket meets that standard and would sell at the rail's higher price.

**B, strongest form:** The jacket belongs on the ordinary rail. On this shop's rail, vintage means made before 1980, because the rail was set up for the older pieces people come in looking for. Customers who know the rail trust that line, and putting a 1996 jacket there would weaken that trust [inferred].

### Common ground
- The jacket was made in 1996.
- It would probably sell on either rail.
- The rail's sign should match what customers expect it to mean.

### The crux
**Double crux · Definitional**
> Whether "vintage" on the shop's rail means at least twenty years old or made before 1980.

- A holds: vintage means at least twenty years old, as most second-hand sellers use the word.
- B holds: on this rail, vintage means made before 1980, the line the rail was set up around.
- If A accepted B's view here: A would agree the jacket belongs on the ordinary rail; if B accepted A's: B would agree it belongs on the vintage rail.
- What would move it: Replace the shared word with two labelled terms, *vintage-by-age* (twenty or more years old) and *vintage-by-cutoff* (made before 1980), so the shop can decide which one the rail's sign uses.

### Secondary cruxes
- **Double crux · Empirical**: Whether the customers who use this rail read the sign as twenty or more years old or as pre-1980. A holds: customers expect the twenty-year meaning; B holds: the rail's regulars trust the pre-1980 line; if accepted: either side's case for its own meaning would weaken; what would move it: asking a sample of the rail's shoppers what they expect "vintage" to mean there, and checking whether the shop has a written rule or past practice for the rail.

### Not actually in dispute
- The jacket's age: both accept 1996.
- Whether it will sell: both expect it to sell on either rail, so saleability doesn't settle the question.
- The higher price on the vintage rail: B doesn't dispute it. It only matters once the meaning of the word is settled.

### Confidence
High: both positions are given in full, the facts are shared, and each conclusion follows directly from which meaning of "vintage" applies.
