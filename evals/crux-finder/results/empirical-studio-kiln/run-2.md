**A, strongest form:** The bill should be split by use, not down the middle. The kiln is by far the biggest load on the meter, and B fires it most weeks while A fires it about once a fortnight. An even split has A paying for B's firings.

**B, strongest form:** An even split is simpler and fair because most of the bill is heaters and lights, which both use equally. The well-insulated kiln runs only a few hours per firing, so it was a small share of last quarter's bill, even with B's firings.

- B fires the kiln more often than A.
- If the kiln used most of the bill, B should pay more.

### The crux
**Double crux · Empirical**
