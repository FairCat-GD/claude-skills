**A, strongest form:** The hall should install panels this year. After the grant, its £6,000 share will be repaid well inside ten years, because the hall is busy in the daytime and every unit generated replaces one bought at current high prices.
**B, strongest form:** The hall should not install the panels. Most output will come in summer, when the playgroup and art class break up and the hall is quietest, and power sent to the grid earns little, so ten-year savings will fall short of £6,000.
