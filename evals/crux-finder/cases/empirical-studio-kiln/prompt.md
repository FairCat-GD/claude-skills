Two potters who share a studio disagree about how to split the electricity bill. Where is the real disagreement?

A: The bill should be split by how much each of us uses, not down the middle. I fire the kiln about once a fortnight; you fire it most weeks. The kiln is by far the biggest thing on the meter, so most of last quarter's electricity went on your firings. Splitting the bill evenly means I am paying for your kiln.

B: Splitting evenly is simpler, and it is fair, because most of the bill is the heaters and the lights, and we use those the same. The kiln is well insulated and only runs for a few hours a firing, so it was a small share of last quarter's electricity, even with my firings. If it really had used most of the bill, I would agree to pay more. It didn't.
