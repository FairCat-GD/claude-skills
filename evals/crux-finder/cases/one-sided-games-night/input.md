I co-host a weekly board-game night with a friend, and we can't agree on money. I think we should start charging £2 at the door. The room at the community centre costs £30 a night, and at the moment the two of us pay it out of our own pockets. Most weeks about twenty people come, so £2 each would cover the room with a little over for new games. I don't think £2 would put anyone off, since it is less than a cup of coffee, and if I thought it would keep new people away I would go along with my co-host's idea. My co-host is convinced that charging will put newcomers off, that someone coming for the first time won't want to pay just to see what it is like, and wants a donations jar instead, even if that means we keep topping up the room money ourselves. Where do we really disagree?
