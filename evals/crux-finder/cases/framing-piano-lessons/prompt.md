Where do A and B really disagree? They are two parents deciding whether their eleven-year-old daughter should carry on with piano lessons.

A: She should stop at the end of this term. She dreads Tuesday evenings, she argues about practising every night, and last week she told her teacher she hates it. I am not saying she hasn't learned anything; she plainly has. But a child should look forward to the things she does after school, and she does not look forward to this.

B: She should carry on. A year ago she could barely read music; now she plays whole pieces with both hands, and she passed her Grade 2 exam with a merit. I know she complains about practising, and I am not pretending she enjoys it; she doesn't. But lessons are for learning, she is learning fast, and stopping now would throw that away.
