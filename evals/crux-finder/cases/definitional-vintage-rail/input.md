Where do these two actually disagree? A and B volunteer at the same charity shop, and they have gone back and forth all week about one denim jacket.

A: The jacket should go on the vintage rail. It is from 1996; the label and the care tag both say so. Vintage means at least twenty years old. That is how most second-hand sellers use the word, and it is what customers expect when they see the sign. Things on the vintage rail are priced higher, and this jacket would sell there.

B: The jacket belongs on the ordinary rail. I don't doubt it is from 1996, and I agree it would probably sell on either rail. But vintage, on our rail, means made before 1980. That is what the rail was set up for: older pieces that people come in looking for, and the customers who know the rail trust that line. A jacket from 1996 is just second-hand.
