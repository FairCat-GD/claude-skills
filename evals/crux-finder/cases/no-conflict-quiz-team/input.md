Where do A and B really disagree? They are two members of a four-person team in a weekly pub quiz league.

A: We need to sign a reserve. Twice this season one of us has been away and we have played with three, and we lost both times. The league lets a team register a fifth player, who would play only on nights when one of the four of us can't make it. Without one, every holiday or late shift costs us points.

B: The four of us should play every match we can. We have been together three seasons, we know who is good at what, and we are second in the league with this line-up. I don't want anyone dropped, and I don't want players rotated so that someone else gets a game. Whenever all four of us can make it, the four of us should play.
