What would change either side's mind here? A and B are on the committee of an amateur theatre group with one slot left in its spring season, and each wants a different play in it.

A: We should put on the farce. We have staged it twice before and it sold out both times, and the box office expects the same again, about 900 tickets over the run. Full houses keep our regular audience coming back and give the cast the experience of playing to a packed room. The group exists to put on the shows people want to see, and this is one.

B: We should stage the new play by the writer from our own workshop. I accept the box office's figures: the farce would sell about 900 tickets and the new play about 450. I also accept that either one would cover its costs, so money is not the issue. But no one else is going to give a new local writer a full production, and giving new writing a stage is what a group like ours is for.
