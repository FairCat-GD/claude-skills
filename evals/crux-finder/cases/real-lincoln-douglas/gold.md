A is Douglas, who spoke first. Both rest their conclusions on the founders: Douglas says they "left each State perfectly free" and that the Government can exist on the principles on which they made it; Lincoln says they placed slavery where the public mind rested in the belief that it was in the course of ultimate extinction, by restricting it from new Territories and ending the slave trade, and that the Union can have peace only once it is placed there again. Both accept that the Union has existed divided for about seventy or eighty years. Any wording about what the founders' policy on slavery was is a match. A run whose primary crux is whether the Union can endure permanently half slave and half free counts no for crux and type. What would move it names the historical record, such as the founders' laws on the Territories and the slave trade.
