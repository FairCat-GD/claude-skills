Where do A and B really disagree? They are two members of a community orchard's committee, arguing about the orchard's stall at the Saturday farmers' market.

A: We should stop taking a stall at the farmers' market. I went through last season's takings book week by week, and once the pitch fee is taken off for every Saturday we were there, the stall lost money. The orchard needs every pound it has for the new trees and the irrigation repair. If the stall can't cover its own pitch, it is costing us money we don't have, however nice it is to see people buying our apple juice.

B: We should keep the stall. I went through the same season from the bank statements, and after every pitch fee the stall came out ahead. Not by much, but ahead. I agree the orchard needs every pound for the trees and the irrigation, and I'd drop the stall tomorrow if it lost money. It doesn't. Selling the juice ourselves still gets us a better price than anyone else pays us for it.
